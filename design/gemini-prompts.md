# Gemini prompts — FSOS + Seeding (3 variations, ref-grounded)

Constraints baked into all three: it's a **nav-driven web app** (not a scroll site) — nav stays
**on top or on the side** and must expose the real functionalities: **Dashboard · Content Tracker ·
Growth · 6-Day Tracker · Tickets · Seeding**. Each prompt renders **two screens in one design
system** — an FSOS dashboard and a Seeding page — so they read as one product. Use the real labels
and numbers (Ecosystem reach 828.9M; Seeding revenue ₹10.8L, teams OWLED Core/Snoball/Hooc/AY,
deals Notion/Zoho/CRED). End every prompt with: *"16:9, high-fidelity realistic UI, sharp text,
no lorem — both screens share identical nav, type, color and components."*

---

## VARIATION A — "Editorial" (Euveka-inspired · elegant, simple)

Design a high-end, editorial dark web-app dashboard, two screens in one system. Warm near-black
canvas (#141210), a very faint dotted "+" technical grid in the background, enormous negative
space. Display type is an elegant high-contrast **serif** (Didot/Canela feel) used large and thin;
tiny uppercase letter-spaced labels for eyebrows; a refined lowercase serif wordmark. Palette is
restrained — warm black, soft cream ink #ece7dd, a single muted accent. Feels like a luxury brand,
calm and confident.

Navigation: a **minimal top bar** — serif wordmark left, a short set of section links right
(Dashboard · Trackers · Growth · Seeding · Tickets), a small round action button in the corner.

Screen 1 — FSOS Dashboard: a large serif hero line, a centered floating data-ring for total
ecosystem reach (828.9M), and a few quiet cards with monospace numbers.
Screen 2 — Seeding Overview: same serif hero ("Seeding"), a refined revenue figure (₹10.8L) with a
thin collection ring, a slim deals list. Luxury, spacious, editorial.

---

## VARIATION B — "Kinetic" (Midu-inspired · bold, creative)

Design a bold, kinetic dark web-app, two screens in one system. Pure black #050505 with a **giant
wordmark glowing through the background** — a soft red-to-purple nebula bleeding through enormous
cut-out letters at the bottom of the hero. Headlines in a heavy geometric sans (Neue Montreal /
General Sans feel), tight and confident; a text-emphasis device where key phrases are bright white
and the rest is dimmed grey. Accent: your purple #8b5cf6 with a hot red glow. Small meta strip
(live clock, "London/IST", status). Energetic, premium, motion-implied.

Navigation: a **floating centered pill menu** at the top ("⋮⋮ Menu" + a live context label like
"4 active deals"), wordmark top-left, a sparkle "＋ New" button top-right. The pill opens the
functionalities (Dashboard, Content Tracker, Growth, 6-Day, Tickets, Seeding).

Screen 1 — FSOS Dashboard: giant "FRONTSEAT" wordmark with the nebula glow through it, a bold
headline, and stat blocks (reach, reels, posts).
Screen 2 — Seeding Overview: giant "SEEDING" wordmark same glow, bold revenue headline (₹10.8L),
and deal stat blocks with purple accents. Bold, cinematic, alive.

---

## VARIATION C — "Atmosphere" (Wishlabs-inspired · dreamy, creative)

Design a premium atmospheric dark web-app, two screens in one system. Hero is a **dreamy gradient
sky** (deep purple → pink → blue, #6a5bf5 → #e58bd0 → #9db4ff) with a soft surreal rendered
cloud/mountain landscape at dusk; a **huge chunky rounded display wordmark in white sits over it
with depth** — the landscape peeks through the letters. A small "Welcome to" label and a one-line
tagline. Below the hero the page turns to a dark near-black work surface with rounded cards, purple
accent, magenta/green data — so it's atmospheric up top, functional below.

Navigation: a **slim left side rail** of clean line icons (Dashboard, Trackers, Growth, 6-Day,
Tickets, Seeding) that expands to labels on hover — OR a minimal top nav; active item marked in
purple. Identity chip top-right.

Screen 1 — FSOS Dashboard: gradient-sky hero with a big rounded "FRONTSEAT" wordmark, then dark
cards below (reach ring 828.9M, growth chart, leaderboard).
Screen 2 — Seeding Overview: same gradient-sky hero with "SEEDING", then dark cards (revenue ₹10.8L
ring, deal-flow chart, team leaderboard, deals table). Dreamy hero, sharp workspace.

---

### How to choose
- **A Editorial** = simplest, most premium, safest. **B Kinetic** = boldest, most "designed."
- **C Atmosphere** = closest to your current nebula look, dreamy but still functional.
All keep a real nav so you can reach every functionality.
